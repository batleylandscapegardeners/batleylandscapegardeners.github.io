Fencing

Fences that stay up in a Yorkshire gale.

Storm-damaged panels, leaning posts, a boundary that's seen better days - replaced properly with quality timber and posts set deep, so you're not doing it again in two years.

Get a free quote

What's involved

Old fencing comes down and goes away. New posts are set in concrete at the right depth - the part that decides how long a fence lasts. Choose concrete posts and gravel boards for maximum life, or timber posts to keep costs down. Panels, closeboard, trellis tops and gates all fitted, including on sloped gardens where panels need stepping.

Typical cost

Expect roughly £60 to £90 per panel bay supplied and fitted with concrete posts, less with timber. Gates from around £120 fitted. Repairs to storm damage priced per job.

Good to know

Fences over two metres need planning consent - and it's one metre next to a highway. Not sure whose fence it is? The fencers we work with will talk you through the usual boundary conventions before anything goes up.
