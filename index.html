Local knowledge matters

Batley's gardens come with their own quirks - sloped plots above the valley, heavy clay soil, and terraced yards that need clever use of space. Planning rules matter too: fences over two metres, and most fencing next to a highway over one metre, need Kirklees Council consent. A good gardener checks before digging or building - the ones we work with do this as standard.

Big job or small one? Gardeners on our books take everything from a one-off hedge trim to a full garden transformation. The quote is always free.
