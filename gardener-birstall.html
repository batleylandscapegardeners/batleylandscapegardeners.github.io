Common jobs in Birstall

Every garden is different, but these are the jobs local gardeners get asked for most in Birstall.

New fencing on sloped plots

Birstall's sloped plots mean fences often need stepping or sloped panels - done properly so there are no gaps at the bottom.

Patio and path laying

Family gardens get the most use from a solid patio - sandstone and porcelain laid on proper sub-bases, with falls away from the house.

Lawn makeovers

Worn lawns on new-build estates get aerated, overseeded and fed - or re-turfed where the builder's thin topsoil never gave grass a chance.
