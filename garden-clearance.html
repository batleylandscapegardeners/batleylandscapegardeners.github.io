When a clearance makes sense

The most common reasons local gardeners get called in for a full clearance.

Selling or letting

A cleared garden photographs better and surveys better. One of the cheapest ways to improve a property before it goes on the market.

Moving in

Inherited an overgrown plot with the house? A clearance shows you what you actually have - boundaries, paths, sheds and all.

Starting a redesign

Every good garden makeover starts with a clean slate: vegetation out, levels revealed, problems visible before the design begins.

What's included

Cutting back overgrowth, removing brambles and self-seeded saplings, strimming and levelling rough ground, clearing sheds and greenhouses by arrangement, and taking all green waste away for responsible disposal. What's left is a tidy, workable plot.
