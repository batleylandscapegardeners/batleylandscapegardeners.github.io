Lawn Care & Turfing

A lawn worth walking on barefoot.

Patchy, mossy or worn-out grass can usually be rescued - and where it can't, a new lawn laid on properly prepared ground transforms a garden in a day.

Get a free quote

What's involved

For new turf, the old surface comes off, the ground is levelled, firmed and topped with quality topsoil, then turf goes down the same day it arrives. For rescues, the treatment depends on the problem: scarifying for thatch and moss, hollow-tine aeration for compacted clay (common around Batley), overseeding and a proper feed programme. Regular mowing visits can be arranged too.

Typical cost

New turf laid runs about £8 to £15 per square metre including ground preparation. Lawn renovation treatments from around £60 a visit. Regular mowing from £25 per visit for a typical garden.

Good to know

Moss and patchiness usually mean compaction or shade, not bad grass. On Batley's clay, aeration in spring or autumn makes the single biggest difference - cheap compared to re-turfing.
