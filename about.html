About

Why this service exists.

Finding a gardener who turns up, does the work properly and charges a fair price shouldn't be a gamble. That's the problem we solve.

What we do

Batley Landscape Gardeners is an independent enquiry service. When you get in touch, we match your job with an experienced, insured local gardener or landscaper who covers your area. They visit, they quote in writing, and you decide. We don't carry out the work ourselves - our job is making sure the person who does is worth having.

What we look for in a gardener

Proper insurance

Public liability cover as a baseline - your property and their people are protected.

Written quotes

Itemised, in writing, before any work starts. No vague day-rates that balloon.

Local reputation

Real work in real Batley gardens, with references that check out.

What it costs you

Nothing. The visit, the quote and our matching are free. Gardeners pay us a fee for introductions - you pay them for the work, at the price you agreed in writing.
