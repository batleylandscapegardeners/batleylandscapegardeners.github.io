Decking

A deck that earns its place in the garden.

Sloping lawns, awkward corners, a patch that never quite dries out - a well-built deck turns the difficult part of the garden into the part everyone uses.

Get a free quote

Timber or composite?

Both done properly, on a solid frame with the right ventilation and fixings.

Timber decking

The classic look at a lower upfront cost. Pressure-treated boards on a treated frame, finished with the right screws and gaps so it sheds water and stays solid.

Composite decking

Higher upfront cost, very little upkeep - no staining, no rot, no algae scrubbing every spring. A good choice for shaded Yorkshire gardens that stay damp.

Built to last, not just to look good on day one

The frame is where decks succeed or fail: posts set properly, joists spaced for the board type, airflow underneath, and water directed away from the house. Steps, edges and balustrades finished cleanly so the whole thing feels like part of the garden, not an afterthought.
