Guide

Winter is easier on a garden that's ready for it.

A West Yorkshire winter tests every fence post, lawn edge and patio joint. An hour of preparation in autumn saves a repair bill in spring - here's what to check.

Lawns

Last mow on a higher setting, leaves cleared before they mat, and a scarify or aeration if the ground is compacted. Spring grass starts with autumn care.

Hedges & shrubs

Final trim before the frost so cut faces harden off, and any dead or crossing branches out before gales find them.

Fences & gates

Push every post - if it moves, it fails in the first storm. Loose panels and rusty fixings are cheap to fix now, expensive after they've blown through a greenhouse.

Patios and paving

Sweep and wash down so moss doesn't turn to ice-rink slime, re-point any open joints before frost gets into them, and make sure water runs away from the house. If slabs already rock or puddles sit against the wall, that's a repair to book before winter, not after.
